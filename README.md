# CineFlo for Claude

Plan a film production with Claude in your [CineFlo](https://cineflo.com) projects. This plugin pairs the CineFlo connector with skills that know how a production is prepared, so Claude works the way a first AD, a script supervisor or a line producer would, not just tool by tool.

## What it does

- **Build a shot list.** Claude checks how the project will be shot (coverage style, cameras, lenses, aspect ratio), asks once if that isn't known yet, reads the scene, and proposes coverage in your shot numbering before saving it.
- **Break down the script.** Claude tags cast, props, wardrobe, vehicles, effects and the rest, scene by scene, reusing the elements already in your breakdown.
- **Schedule shoot days.** Claude groups unscheduled scenes by location, time of day and cast, and lays them onto shoot days on the stripboard.
- **Prep a call sheet.** Claude drafts a day's call sheet from the stripboard, with call times, cast statuses and locations. It never sends it: you send from CineFlo.
- **Review the budget.** Claude reads the cost report and explains where you are over, where money is committed and what is left.
- **Start a project.** Claude creates a project and gives you the link to import your screenplay.

## Use it

1. Add the plugin, then connect CineFlo from the plugin's **Connectors** tab and sign in with your CineFlo account. Connected Apps is part of CineFlo Pro.
2. On the CineFlo sign-in page, choose which projects and which tabs Claude can view or edit. You can change this any time in CineFlo under Settings → Privacy & Data → Connected Apps.
3. Ask in plain words, for example "Build a shot list for scene 12 of Night Shift" or "Which scenes aren't scheduled yet?", or pick a skill from the `/` menu.

## Data

The skills are instructions only: this plugin contains no scripts and runs nothing on your computer. Claude reaches your projects through the CineFlo connector at `https://mcp.cineflo.com/mcp`, which is run by CineFlo (Neon Media LLC) and only reaches the projects and tabs you share with it. Contact details, pay rates and uploaded files are never shared with Claude, nothing is sent to anyone, and anything deleted can be restored in CineFlo for 30 days. See [How Connected Apps work](https://cineflo.com/connected-apps/) and the [CineFlo privacy policy](https://cineflo.com/privacy-policy/).
